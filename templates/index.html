THE DETAILS

Questions, answered.

Do I need a website already?+

No. Your custom website is included in the plan. If you already have a site, we’ll discuss the best way to connect your assistant.

Does it confirm appointments?+

It gathers the enquiry, preferred timing and contact details, then sends them to you. You confirm the appointment. It never promises a slot it can’t see.

How long does it take?+

Usually a few days after our first chat. I build your site and train the assistant, then send you a private preview to try before it goes live.

What if I want to cancel?+

You can cancel anytime. When your subscription ends, your hosted website and assistant are paused. See the full terms for the details.

Is it right for my business?+

It’s made for local service businesses: trades, salons, barbers, cleaners, garages, dog groomers and more. If customers ask questions before getting in touch, let’s talk.

Where do the enquiries go?+

Straight to your email inbox as a clear summary with the customer’s contact details. Messages are processed by our AI provider to generate replies. Read our privacy policy for how information is handled.

Do I have to manage any of this?+

I look after the build, hosting and updates. You tell me when your services or prices change, and follow up with the enquiries.
